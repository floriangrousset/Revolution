# Portrait Attributions

Portraits whose source is a **GPO bioguide id** are official congressional
portraits published by the U.S. Government Publishing Office. As works of the
U.S. federal government they are in the **public domain**. The files are
mirrored from the [unitedstates/images](https://github.com/unitedstates/images)
project (`https://unitedstates.github.io/images/congress/450x550/<BIOGUIDE>.jpg`).

Portraits whose source is a **Wikimedia Commons URL** are freely licensed as
noted below; the required attribution (author + license) is recorded per row
and embedded in each persona's `image_attribution` field.

These images identify the public figures the simulation's personas approximate.
Their use implies no endorsement of this project by the subjects, the
photographers, or the U.S. government.
